10.5 m Who I am

About

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of Utah, advised by Dr. Shahrzad Roshankhah, graduating in May 2027. My research combines hydraulic fracturing experiments (digital image correlation, acoustic emission), FDEM numerical modeling in Irazú, poroelastic analysis, and dimensional analysis to understand fracture evolution and instability in naturally fractured rock.

Before my Ph.D., I completed an M.Tech in Geotechnical Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar, worked as a geotechnical design engineer at Larsen & Toubro on metro TBM tunnels and ground improvement, and was a project assistant at IIT Bombay working on the Material Point Method. I serve as President of the ARMA University of Utah Student Chapter, and I have been selected for an NSF–DOE-funded research internship in geothermal geomechanics at Los Alamos National Laboratory.

24.0 m Current work

Research

Connected threads linking laboratory fracture experiments, numerical modeling, analytical frameworks, and apparatus design.

01 · Experiments + Numerical Modeling

Hydraulic Fracturing & FDEM Modeling of Naturally Fractured Rocks

Pairing physical hydraulic-fracturing experiments with FDEM numerical modeling in Irazú to understand breakdown pressure and fracture interaction where an induced fracture meets pre-existing natural fractures. Fracture-network models built from DFNs extracted from Utah FORGE field data show that breakdown pressure depends non-monotonically on natural fracture density.

DICAcoustic EmissionFDEM / IrazúXFEMDFN
02 · Analytical Framework

Dimensionless Poroelastic Framework for Fluid Injection

A six-group dimensionless framework (Πpmax, D, Td, κ, ω, λ) built from first principles for dual-permeability poroelastic systems, applied to Dakota and Entrada sandstones to classify injection regimes and leak-off behavior.

PoroelasticityMATLABDimensional Analysis
03 · Apparatus Design

High-Temperature Split-Ring Confinement Testing

Design and fabrication of a split-ring confinement apparatus for thermal crack testing of granite disc specimens under plane-strain conditions with DIC imaging, targeting temperatures representative of geothermal reservoir conditions.

SolidWorks / FEAThermal TestingDIC Imaging
04 · Rock Slope Stability

Failure Mechanisms in Jointed Rock Slopes

Calibrated numerical simulations that separate the roles of rock matrix and natural fractures in slope instability, and expose limitations of conventional strength reduction methods. Published in Rock Mechanics and Rock Engineering (2025).

Strength ReductionJointed RockNumerical Modeling
